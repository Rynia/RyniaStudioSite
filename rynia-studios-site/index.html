02 — Origin

From nickname to studio.

Rynia was never meant to be a studio name. Years ago, it was simply a personal nickname— a digital identity I carried through various projects and communities. Over time, it became more than that: a symbol of consistency, discipline, and creative persistence.

When I committed to game development as a serious, long-term practice, the choice was natural. Rynia Studios isn't a company building games. It's a person with a vision, building games with intention, learning in public, and evolving through every iteration.

Short Description

Kombo Kart is a mobile auto-battler card game where drafting, card evolution, and positioning decide every battle.

Kombo Kart follows a simple loop: draft your cards, evolve them, choose their position, then watch the battle play out automatically. The philosophy behind the game is simple—few cards, many decisions. As development continues, the drafting pool and elemental synergies will expand to give every run its own shape.
