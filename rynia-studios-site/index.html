02 — Origin

From a Nickname to a Developer Studio.

Rynia didn't begin with deep or mystical meanings. Around 2019, it started simply from a search for a nickname that would fit naturally and stay with me for years. Over time, in every server, project, and community, my identity became Rynia.

I later learned it was the name of a village in Poland, but for me it was never a location—it was my digital identity forged across years online. When founding Rynia Studios, carrying this name was natural: it reminds me never to leave things unfinished, to build patiently layer by layer, and to evolve step by step. What started as a simple online handle has now transformed into the name of an independent studio where I build software, digital products, and games.
