01 / Manifesto

Five principles.
Zero compromises.

Applied in every release
  1. 01

    Local-first, by default.

    Data lives on your device. It stays on your device. If an app can work offline, it must work offline. The cloud is a feature, never a requirement.

  2. 02

    Zero tracking. Zero telemetry.

    No analytics SDKs. No crash reporters that phone home. No "anonymous usage data." Your behaviour is not a data point for us to optimise.

  3. 03

    Craft over velocity.

    We ship when it's right, not when a roadmap says so. Every animation, every word, every pixel is deliberate. Speed without polish is noise.

  4. 04

    Software as a public good.

    Core functionality is never paywalled. Monetisation, when it happens, targets comfort — never access. No dark patterns, no artificial scarcity.

  5. 05

    Sustainably built.

    Small binaries. Low power draw. Long device compatibility. Software that ages gracefully respects both the planet and your hardware.
